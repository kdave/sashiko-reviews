regular superblock offsets at btrfs_sb_offset(i)?

And fs/btrfs/block-group.c:btrfs_exclude_super_stripes() rejects any zoned
block group that overlaps btrfs_sb_offset(i).

While btrfs_sb_offset(0) (64 KiB) falls inside zone 0, btrfs_sb_offset(1)
(64 MiB) falls outside the primary mirror's two log zones whenever
zinfo->zone_size is smaller than 64 MiB. In addition, btrfs_sb_offset(2)
(256 GiB) never overlaps the log-structured superblock zones at 0B, 512 GiB,
or 4 TiB for any supported zone size.

Without excluding those non-overlapping regular superblock zones, won't
btrfs_calc_avail_data_space() (and btrfs_zoned_data_chunk_allowed() later in
this series) over-report available space by 1 to 2 zones per device?

[Severity: Medium]
Does subtracting sb_bytes before rounding down to zinfo->zone_size under-count
available space by an extra zone when total is not zone-aligned and ends inside
a superblock log zone?

On zoned filesystems, device->total_bytes is only aligned to
fs_info->sectorsize (for example in btrfs_init_new_device(),
btrfs_grow_device(), and btrfs_shrink_device()), whereas device->bytes_used is
a multiple of zinfo->zone_size.

If total has a trailing partial zone (total = N * zinfo->zone_size + rem, with
0 < rem < zinfo->zone_size) and zone N happens to be a superblock log zone,
zone_start_physical(sb_zone + j, zinfo) < total is true and adds a full
zinfo->zone_size to sb_bytes, even though avail only includes rem bytes from
that partial zone.

Subtracting a full zinfo->zone_size from avail then borrows
zinfo->zone_size - rem bytes from an earlier full allocatable zone, and the
final round_down(avail, zinfo->zone_size) discards the remaining rem bytes,
losing one full allocatable zone from the result.
