[Severity: High]
This isn't a bug introduced by this patch, but pre-existed in
scrub_read_endio() and was carried over into the extracted helper.

Can concurrent bio completions cause a data race on stripe->bitmaps in
scrub_read_endio_common()?

If a single scrub read operation is split into multiple bios (for example,
when crossing extent boundaries), their completions can concurrently invoke
scrub_read_endio_common() from multiple Btrfs worker threads. The
scrub_bitmap_set_io_error() and scrub_bitmap_clear_io_error() functions
expand to non-atomic bitmap_set() and bitmap_clear() read-modify-write
operations.

Since there is no locking around these modifications, could IO error bits be
silently overwritten and lost, causing scrub to miss corrupted data and fail
to repair it?
