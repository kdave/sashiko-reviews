[Severity: Medium]
Can this relocate an append-only block group that was not full when queued on
fs_info->reclaim_bgs and subsequently filled to capacity?

An active, non-full block group can be added to fs_info->reclaim_bgs when
btrfs_update_block_group() sees bg->used cross below the reclaim threshold,
or when do_reclaim_sweep() queues a partially used block group.  Because being
on reclaim_bgs does not mark the block group read-only or stop
do_allocation_zoned() from allocating out of it, new allocations can fill the
zone until bg->alloc_offset reaches bg->zone_capacity before
btrfs_reclaim_block_group() runs.

At that point btrfs_zoned_bg_is_full(bg) is true even though bg->used is at or
near 100% of the zone capacity.  Skipping should_reclaim_block_group() then
relocates a nearly full (or completely full) block group, causing full-zone
write amplification and consuming the synchronous reclaim batch budget in
flush_space() without recovering free zones.
