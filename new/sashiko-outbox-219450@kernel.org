[Severity: High]
Can bitmap_equal() read past the end of device->zone_info->seq_zones?

btrfs_init_dev_replace_tgtdev() only requires bdev_nr_bytes(bdev) to be at
least btrfs_device_get_total_bytes(srcdev), whereas btrfs_get_dev_zone_info()
sizes zone_info->nr_zones and zone_info->seq_zones from the raw block device
capacity bdev_nr_sectors(bdev):

fs/btrfs/zoned.c:btrfs_get_dev_zone_info() {
    ...
	nr_sectors = bdev_nr_sectors(bdev);
	zone_info->zone_size_shift = ilog2(zone_info->zone_size);
	zone_info->nr_zones = nr_sectors >> ilog2(zone_sectors);
	if (!IS_ALIGNED(nr_sectors, zone_sectors))
		zone_info->nr_zones++;
    ...
	zone_info->seq_zones = bitmap_zalloc(zone_info->nr_zones, GFP_KERNEL);
}

If srcdev was formatted or resized to a total_bytes smaller than its
underlying block device and is replaced with a target disk whose physical
size lies between srcdev->total_bytes and bdev_nr_bytes(srcdev->bdev),
device->zone_info->nr_zones will be smaller than srcdev->zone_info->nr_zones.
Passing srcdev->zone_info->nr_zones to bitmap_equal() then reads out of
bounds on device->zone_info->seq_zones and compares zones beyond
srcdev->total_bytes, rejecting valid replacement devices with -EINVAL.
