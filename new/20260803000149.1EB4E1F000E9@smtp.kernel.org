btrfs: zoned: fix use-after-free in btrfs_reset_unused_block_groups()

[Severity: Critical]
This is a pre-existing issue, but does this zone reset sequence have a
time-of-check to time-of-use race?

Looking at btrfs_reset_unused_block_groups(), blkdev_zone_mgmt() is called
with REQ_OP_ZONE_RESET earlier in this loop without holding locks that would
prevent the chunk from being concurrently deleted.

Could a concurrent operation that iterates the chunk tree (such as a balance
racing with memory pressure flushing) discover the empty block group, set
it to read-only, remove the logical chunk, and free the physical device
extents?

If those physical extents are immediately reallocated to a new chunk, could
the delayed REQ_OP_ZONE_RESET wipe the newly allocated zone data when it
reaches the disk?

Since this bg->ro check happens after the physical zone has already been
reset:
