[Severity: Medium]
Can zinfo be dereferenced before the NULL check here?  Initializing total
and total_zones reads zinfo->zone_size and zinfo->zone_size_shift before
checking !zinfo, causing a NULL pointer dereference if device->zone_info is
NULL and allowing the compiler to optimize away the check.

[Severity: Medium]
Can total - device->bytes_used underflow when device->bytes_used is read a
second time without chunk_mutex?

btrfs_calc_avail_data_space() calls btrfs_zoned_dev_avail_bytes() under
rcu_read_lock() without holding chunk_mutex.  Because device->bytes_used is
read twice directly (bypassing btrfs_device_get_bytes_used()), a concurrent
chunk allocation in btrfs_create_chunk() can increase device->bytes_used
past total between the initial check and the subtraction, wrapping avail
near U64_MAX and reporting bogus free space via statfs().  In addition,
overwriting avail discards the value passed in by the caller.
