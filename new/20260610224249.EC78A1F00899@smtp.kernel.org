[Severity: Critical]
This is a pre-existing issue, but are BTRFS_ROOT_REF_KEY and 
BTRFS_ROOT_BACKREF_KEY items actually validated in the tree-checker?

The commit message mentions we have done this in ROOT_REF checks, but looking 
at check_leaf_item() in fs/btrfs/tree-checker.c, it seems handlers for 
BTRFS_ROOT_REF_KEY and BTRFS_ROOT_BACKREF_KEY are completely omitted from the 
switch statement.

stack buffer overflow in btrfs_get_name()?
