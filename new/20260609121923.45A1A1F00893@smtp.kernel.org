[Severity: High]
This is a pre-existing issue, but does this space accounting code correctly
update the unusable zone bytes?

Looking at btrfs_zoned_reserve_data_reloc_bg() during block group migration,
it appears bg->zone_unusable is subtracted from space_info->disk_total. Since
disk_total tracks physical capacity and was already decremented by
bg->length * factor on the previous line, would this double-decrement
shrink disk_total too much and potentially underflow?

Also, by not decrementing space_info->bytes_zone_unusable (e.g., via
btrfs_space_info_update_bytes_zone_unusable), could this leak unusable
zone bytes in the original data_sinfo and lead to premature ENOSPC?
