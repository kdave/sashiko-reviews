[Severity: High]
Could a hole before an unmodified shared next leaf still be missed in
maybe_send_hole() in fs/btrfs/send.c when num_bytes == ram_bytes?

Suppose in parent_root, the first leaf ends with a file extent item at
offset 1M covering [1M, 3M), and the next leaf starts with a file extent
item at offset 3M covering [3M, 4M).

If in send_root (with no-holes) [1M, 2M) is overwritten or cloned with a new
1M extent and [2M, 3M) is hole-punched (or [1M, 3M) is hole-punched and
[1M, 2M) is written), btrfs_drop_extents() and insert_reserved_file_extent()
replace the last item of the first leaf at offset 1M with a new extent
covering [1M, 2M) that has num_bytes == 1M and ram_bytes == 1M, while the
next leaf remains unmodified and shared with parent_root.

During incremental send, btrfs_compare_trees() compares the last item of the
first leaf in both roots (cmp == 0) and calls:

  changed_cb()
    -> changed_extent()
      -> process_extent()
        -> maybe_send_hole()

Because num_bytes == ram_bytes on the new [1M, 2M) extent, maybe_send_hole()
returns early without punching the hole in [2M, 3M). Then
btrfs_compare_trees() advances both left_path and right_path out of the
first leaf, skips the shared next leaf, and finish_inode_if_needed() calls
get_last_extent(sctx, (u64)-1), which finds [3M, 4M) and sets
sctx->cur_inode_last_extent = 4M, leaving the hole in [2M, 3M) unsent.
