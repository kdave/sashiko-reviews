btrfs: zoned: reset active_meta_bg on zone finish

This patch ensures that the active_meta_bg and active_system_bg pointers are
properly reset when a block group finishes its zone. It adds cleanup logic to
do_zone_finish() to prevent the pointers from becoming stale when zone
finishing happens through paths other than the main pivot path.

Fixes: 13bb483d32ab ("btrfs: zoned: activate metadata block group on write time")
