[Severity: Medium]
How does summing avail across all devices and comparing it against
2 * chunk_bytes handle multi-device allocation constraints?

First, chunk allocation requires free zones on specific separate devices
(or contiguous zones on a single device for DUP), not just an aggregate byte
sum across alloc_list. For example, on a 2-device RAID1 or RAID0 filesystem
(chunk_bytes = 2 * zone_size), if device 1 has 1 * zone_size free and
device 2 has 3 * zone_size free (due to different device sizes or SINGLE
metadata chunks), avail is 4 * zone_size >= 2 * chunk_bytes. Won't
btrfs_zoned_data_chunk_allowed() allow do_chunk_alloc() to consume the last
zone on device 1, leaving relocation unable to allocate a target chunk?
