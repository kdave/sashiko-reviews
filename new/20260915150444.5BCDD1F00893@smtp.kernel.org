btrfs: clear free space tree state on rebuild failure

btrfs_rebuild_free_space_tree() sets BTRFS_FS_CREATING_FREE_SPACE_TREE
and BTRFS_FS_FREE_SPACE_TREE_UNTRUSTED before rebuilding the tree. This
commit clears both flags on all free space tree rebuild failure paths to
prevent affecting delayed reference processing and free-space caching.

[Severity: Critical]
Could clearing these safety flags on a live, non-aborted filesystem
lead to data corruption?

If btrfs_start_transaction() fails (e.g. due to ENOMEM or disk exhaustion
during a remount with clear_cache) and the filesystem isn't aborted, clearing
these flags falsely signals that the partially destroyed tree is trusted.

Caching threads might then read incorrect free space information from
the corrupted tree, leading to allocations over existing data, and
delayed refs could further corrupt the tree.
