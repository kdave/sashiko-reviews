btrfs: zoned: don't activate conventional zones of mixed block groups

This commit modifies btrfs_load_block_group_zone_info() to prevent conventional
zones in mixed block groups from incorrectly consuming active zone resources.
It achieves this by letting conventional zones inherit the activeness of the
first sequential zone at mount time.

[Severity: Medium]

mount time, does the runtime activation path in btrfs_zone_activate() still
suffer from the same issue?

Looking at btrfs_zone_activate(), it unconditionally iterates over all stripes
and consumes active zone tokens even for conventional zones:
